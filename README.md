# No Sports

A Chrome add-on that hides sports content across the web.

Tired of scores, trades, and game talk showing up everywhere? No Sports quietly removes it from the pages you visit.

## What it does

- **Hides sports posts and links** on X, Reddit, YouTube, and most news sites.
- **Hides sports tabs, boxes, and trends**, like the NFL box in the X sidebar.
- **Covers up full sports pages**, like ESPN or a sports subreddit, with a "Show it anyway" button in case you need one.
- **Lets you add your own words** to block, for teams or names that still get through.
- **Can be turned off for one site** from its menu.

It runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect anything, and it does not send anything anywhere.

## How to install

Chrome only allows one-click installs from its own store, so this takes about a minute:

1. On this page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip the file. You will get a folder called `no-sports-main`.
3. In Chrome, go to `chrome://extensions`.
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right corner).
5. Click **Load unpacked** (top left) and choose the folder you unzipped.

That's it. Open a new tab and sports should be gone. Tabs that were already open need a refresh.

## How to use it

Click the puzzle piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then **No Sports**. From there you can:

- Switch blocking on or off.
- Turn it off for the site you are on.
- Add extra words to block, one per line.

## Good to know

- It works by spotting sports words, so it will sometimes miss a post, or hide something that is not about sports. If a team or name keeps slipping through, add it to the extra words box.
- Websites change how they are built. If something stops being hidden, the add-on may need an update.

## How to update

Download the ZIP again, replace your old folder with the new one, then click the round arrow on the No Sports card at `chrome://extensions`.
